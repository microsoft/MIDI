Windows MIDI Services is Here

Everyone on supported Windows 11 releases (currently 25h2 and later) who has updates enabled has the new Windows MIDI Services already in Windows. Windows MIDI Services brings enhanced MIDI 1.0 support (including multi-client and built-in loopbacks) to your PC, and enables the MIDI 2.0 infrastructure including a new driver.

For developers and technical customers, you may download the latest preview releases from GitHub. Everyone else should wait until the software is delivered with Windows 11.
