  1. Generators are clips. They launch, loop, link and sit on the timeline, and they play to the track's destination. Nothing new to route or clock.
  2. Euclidean rhythm. Each ring spreads its pulses as evenly as it can over its steps, and Rotate turns the ring. Three rings on three notes give an interlocking part from one clip.
  3. Chance melody. Picks notes from a scale inside a range. Keep from the last pass decides how much changes each time around, so a part can drift slowly instead of jumping.
  4. Arpeggiator. Plays what you hold on the track's source, or the notes another track is playing. Order, rate, octaves and length are the usual controls.
  5. Repeatable. Every result comes from the clip's seed and the pass number. Jumping back replays the same notes, and export writes what you heard. Same plays one fixed result on every pass.
  6. Make notes writes passes into an ordinary notes clip you can edit by hand. The sequence remembers that those notes came from a generator (see comp 8).
