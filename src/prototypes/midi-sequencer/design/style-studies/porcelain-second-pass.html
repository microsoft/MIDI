Porcelain, second pass

Porcelain with the chrome made one flat surface, as you asked. The original Porcelain is unchanged; this is a layer over it (porcelain-2.css). What changed:

  • The transport is part of the window. No raised deck: its round keys stand straight up from the window, like the title bar's buttons.
  • The track headers sit flush. They're no longer raised blocks. The selected track is lit rather than pressed in. The keys on them (M, S, and R) and the clips in the launcher stay raised.
  • The timeline is one well for all the tracks, or no well at all, side by side below, for you to pick. In the well, faint engraved lines separate the tracks; with no well, the grid is engraved in the surface itself.
  • The clip editor is part of the window too, with no raised frame. An engraved line sets it apart from the busy arrange above. The piano roll keeps its own well, like a screen set into the case.

You approved no well, with the timeline a step darker, like the piano roll; it's now on a field in the roll's color. See it dark, and in your window color.
