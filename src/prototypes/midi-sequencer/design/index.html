Windows MIDI Sequencer, design comps

A MIDI 2.0 sequencer that records and plays MIDI only, with clip launching, step patterns, generators and an ordinary timeline in one window. These are HTML: open any page in a browser, or run capture.ps1 for pictures in shots and check.ps1 for a layout check. The written design is MIDI-Sequencer-design.md in this folder. Nothing here is built.

1. The main window
Tracks, pinned rows, folders, the clip launcher beside the timeline, automation on a track, recording into a slot, and the clip editor at the bottom.
2. A notes clip in its own window
Piano roll with MIDI 2.0 per-note pitch bend, exact pitch, 16-bit velocity, per-note pan, ghost notes and a scale.
3. Pattern clips
The step sequencer: lanes with their own length, chance, ratchets, conditions, nudges and locks, plus a melodic mode.
4. Generator clips
Euclidean rhythm, a chance melody that drifts from pass to pass, and an arpeggiator.
5. Where a track records from and plays to
Source and destination, echo while armed, MIDI 2.0 or MIDI 1.0 per destination, start-up messages, missing devices.
6. Clock and sync
Internal tempo or another device's clock, clock out with offsets, MIDI 2.0 tempo messages, MIDI Time Code out.
7. Getting MIDI in and out
Standard MIDI Files, captures from MIDI Monitor and the console, paste, and MIDI 2.0 clip file export.
8. About this project
Author and license like MIDI Glass and MIDI Patchbay, plus where each clip came from.
