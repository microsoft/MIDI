  1. Record from. A device, a group and a channel, or any channel. Choose what gets recorded. System exclusive is off unless you turn it on, so a device's chatter doesn't fill your clips.
  2. Echo while armed. What you play goes straight to the track's destination, translated the same way playback is, so you hear the sound you're recording for.
  3. Play to. A device, a group and a channel. The list shows how each device is connected and whether it speaks MIDI 2.0. The app sends MIDI 2.0 or MIDI 1.0 to match what the device's function block says, because Windows doesn't translate based on function blocks.
  4. Missing devices. Moog One is unplugged. Bass keeps it, plays nothing to it, and picks it up again when it's back. Nothing silently goes somewhere else.
  5. When playback starts. Program, bank and any other set-up messages go out before the first note. Program names come from the device over MIDI-CI when it offers them.
  6. Loopbacks. To feed another app (a DAW, a lighting program), play to a loopback. The sequencer can make one from this list.
