Why it reads at a glance on a dark stage. The plate is 86 % opaque over the deck, so a themed or photographic background shows through faintly — that is the "glass". Rim, pipe and glow all come from one hue, so a whole surface can be re-themed by swapping six colors.

The one rule that holds the language together: a control never uses more than one hue, and a hue never appears anywhere except the rim, the value pipe and the bloom. That is what keeps a busy surface readable and makes a theme swap a six-color operation. Sizes here are at 100 %; every measurement is a multiple of 4 px so grid snapping always lands cleanly.
