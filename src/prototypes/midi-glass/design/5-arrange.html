Repeat is the single biggest time saver in the whole app. Building a sixteen channel mixer by hand means sixteen copies and sixteen hand-edited channel numbers, and one of them will be wrong. Here you build one strip, say what should step — channel, controller number, note, group or nothing — and the bank comes out correct. The same rule fills the label from {n}.

Snapping rules. Controls snap to the grid, to each other's edges and centers, and to the page margins. Holding Alt suspends snapping while you drag. Arrow keys nudge by one pixel, Shift+arrow by one grid cell. There is deliberately no free rotation on a control: a rotated fader is hard to hit and impossible to describe to a screen reader. Text and images can be turned in 90° steps, which is all a vertical strip label needs.
