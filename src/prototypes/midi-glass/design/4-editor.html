Edit and Try are one keystroke apart. Ctrl+Enter flips the whole canvas live without leaving the editor, so a customer can feel a fader and then nudge it two pixels. The monitor rail at the bottom is filtered to the selected control by default — that is the fastest way to answer "is this thing actually sending what I think it is", and it is the same message view the MIDI Monitor tool uses. Nothing here is modal: the inspector always reflects the selection, and an empty selection shows the page's own properties instead.
