Screen 32 — Good Form, after the rational industrial design of the sixties and the small instruments that took it up again. A warm light gray panel with a fine bead-blasted grain, light keys that stand on it, knobs whose caps are their color, slim dark fader slots, and small black windows for anything that moves. Words are printed above the controls, the way a panel is printed. A key is light gray until it is on, and then it is its color. It asks for almost nothing new: two of its three asks are the same ones Insert Coin makes.

Why the six colors are all mid-dark. The panel is light, so every color had to be dark enough to read on it: a cap or a lit key measures 3.48 to 4.84 : 1 against the panel, and 4.29 to 5.81 once it goes a shade deeper when on, which is also what lets a white name read on it (5.45 to 7.39). That puts all six at about the same lightness, so the closest pair for a color-blind player is orange and mustard: delta E 8.3 for a green-blind (deutan) player and 8.7 for a red-blind (protan) one. A key at rest is only 1.11 : 1 against the panel; its soft shadow is what separates it, as on Bone.

Built — what the engine already does for this panel

A warm light panel with a fine bead-blasted grain (deck gradient, grain speckle). Light keys held apart by a soft warm shadow (shadowColor, shadowSpread), as on Bone. Words printed above knobs and faders (labels above) and inside pads (namesInsideSwitches). On is the color outright, a shade deeper so a white name reads (fillWhenOnPercent 100, onLiftPercent −12). A thin arc (arcThickness) and printed marks round every knob (knobTickCount 11), a white line on the cap (pointerOnCap, pointerColor). Slim dark slots with no fill and a light cap with a wide line of its color (faderPlate none, faderFillPercent 0, capLineWide), and a printed scale (faderScalePercent). A gray slot for gray keys (neutralColor). Charcoal rules between groups (ruleColor, ruleFades off), captions with no panel (panelFill none). Black windows (wellColor, wellFillsControl), and LEDs in dark holders (lampShape dot, lampHolderColor).

Needs — two shared asks and one small one

A knob's cap is one color for the whole theme; here it is the knob's own color. Every key is a rounded rectangle; the transport keys here are round. Insert Coin, screen 28, asks for both, so building them once serves both themes. Numbers in a window take the control's color, and in a black window those six colors are 3.02 to 4.21 : 1, fine for a line and too faint for small numbers. Here the numbers are light.

What it costs

It is a light theme, which is bright in a dark room and on a dark stage. A key at rest is the panel's color (1.11 : 1), and only its shadow holds it apart, so a theme editor must never let that shadow go flat. Pads have no color until they are on: a layout that finds its drums by color at rest wants Insert Coin instead. Orange and mustard are the closest pair: delta E 8.3 for a green-blind (deutan) player and 8.7 for a red-blind (protan) one, so never use those two for opposite jobs. Print reads at 11.15 : 1, dim print at 5.52, light figures in a window at 16.29.

Three asks. The first two are the same ones Insert Coin makes, so building them once serves both themes.

AskWhy Good Form needs itWithout it
1 Knob caps in the control's color (Insert Coin's ask 1). The cap takes the control's own color, and the line on it picks white or charcoal, whichever reads: white on every color here (4.42 to 6.15 : 1), charcoal on the gray cap (12.43). On the small instruments this theme nods to, a knob's color is its cap, and a row of colored caps is the first thing anybody notices. Every cap one color. The knob's color shows only in its thin arc.
2 Round keys (Insert Coin's ask 3). Here with no ring round the key and no color at rest (switchFillAtRest 0): a light round key standing on the panel, its color when on, a pixel down when held. Round transport keys standing on a flat panel are the other half of the look. Rounded square keys, which this theme already uses for pads.
3 Figures in a window in their own ink. Numbers inside a dark window drawn in an ink the theme names, while lines and bars there keep the control's color. Six mid-dark colors that read on a light panel are too dark for small numbers on black: 3.02 to 4.21 : 1. Light figures measure 16.29. Numbers in the control's color, under 4.5 : 1 in the window. The engine draws them that way on purpose today.

What I left out on purpose. A speaker grille, screws, and any maker's layout. Players of those instruments will recognize the family: the light panel, the colored caps, the black window, the lowercase words. But there is no copied arrangement here. The four colored encoders in a row that one famous synthesizer has are not on this page; the colored caps are two by two, in other colors. No names or logos. Where I was honest rather than faithful: those instruments print almost nothing, and here every control is named, because a layout is often played by somebody who did not build it. The type is Segoe UI Variable, light for the title; that is the layout's choice, and the theme sets no typeface. Every color was measured.
