35 · Fretboard: a window onto a guitar neck, played with strings instead of pads
Standard tuning, frets 0–12 of 22 showing, strum side Right. The low string is at the bottom. Scale dots show E minor pentatonic, and every fret plays. An E minor shape is held on the low strings, and G on the B string is being bent up a whole step. Dashed outlines are what Add fretboard buttons adds.
Bend: push a finger across its string, up or down, and the note bends up, as far as Bend range (2 semitones here, so G3 reaches A3). The finger carries the string; it never jumps to the next one.
Hammer-ons: a higher fret on a sounding string changes the note without a new attack; lifting goes back to the fret still held. The highest fret held on a string is the one that sounds.
Strum: sweep across the strum area and each string plays at the fret held on it, or open, in the order the finger crosses them. Faster is louder. Strum side Left puts it on the left and turns the neck round, nut on the right.
Neck strip: the whole neck in miniature, under the strings. The box is the frets showing; drag it to move along the neck. Up the neck and Down the neck move it a fret.
