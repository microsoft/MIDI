Free — what Bigwig reuses

The same machinery as the two tonal themes, with the hue wash turned off, so the plate stays a neutral raised gray and orange only ever means "this is the value" or "this is on". Corner rounding down to 3 px, glow at zero, and the six hue slots carrying their palette. No new control types.

Two additions — what it needs

The rim has to be able to be a neutral edge rather than the control's hue, which makes that property three-valued instead of two. And the value strip moves to the top edge, which is one more option beside the existing bottom and none. Both are small, and both are things a customer would ask for anyway.

On the LED ring

Worth doing, with a size floor. A ringed knob is one element, the same as the plain arc, because the lamps are a repeating mask rather than shapes. Lamp count is a number in the theme and does not change with size. But below about 48 px the lamps stop separating and it reads as a fine comb, so the control should fall back to the solid arc on its own rather than making somebody notice and fix it.

What I deliberately left out. The second arc of white lamps under the main ring is on an endless encoder with no pointer, so it is almost certainly showing the value that arc is not — not something our knobs need. Skipped. I also kept the plate a flat gray rather than chasing the very subtle vertical shading on their panels, because that shading is doing the job our translucent plate already does and it would only show up as banding on a projector. Colors here are eyeballed from your images, not sampled from anything of theirs, and the name is yours to keep or drop.
