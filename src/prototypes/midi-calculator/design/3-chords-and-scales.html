  1. Chord to notes. Type any chord the Sequencer's Key and chords row understands ("Dm7/G", "B♭maj7", "F#m7b5", "C/E"), or build it from the three lists. The notes are voiced the way the Sequencer plays them: close position from middle C up, the bass an octave below. Play sends them to the Sound Module or any endpoint.
  2. Name a chord, the reverse. When the notes fit more than one name, all of them are listed, best first. The list of names is the same one the Sequencer accepts, so anything named here can be typed there.
  3. Chords in a key. The Sequencer's scales (major, natural and harmonic minor, the modes, pentatonics and blues), with Roman numerals. Click a chord to send it to Chord to notes.
