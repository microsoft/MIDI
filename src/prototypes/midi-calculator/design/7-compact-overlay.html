  1. Compact overlay: Ctrl+Shift+O, or the button in the main window's title bar. One calculator at a time, sized to fit it. It remembers its size and place separately from the full window, so it goes back beside your music app each time.
  2. Pick the calculator from the title. The list is the calculators that fit a small window. Pages that need a lot of room, like Decode UMP words, stay in the full window.
  3. Tap tempo under the table, so a tempo you tap shows its delay times straight away. The overlay reads and writes the same session values as the full window.
