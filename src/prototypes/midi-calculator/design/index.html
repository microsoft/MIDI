Windows MIDI Calculator

Comps for a small calculator you keep open beside your music app: tempo and delay times, notes and frequencies, chords, timecode, MIDI 2.0 values and Windows timestamps. It's built like the other Windows MIDI tools and uses their shared code. These are HTML: open any page in a browser, or run capture.ps1 for pictures in shots. Nothing here is built. The design, the math rules with their spec sections, and the phases are in README.md.

1. Tempo and beats
Tap tempo, the beat calculator, note lengths for delays, tempo change, measure MIDI Clock.
2. Notes and pitch
Note to frequency and back, intervals, a note table.
3. Chords and scales
Chord to notes, name a chord you play, chords in a key.
4. Timecode and samples
Timecode math at every common frame rate, samples and buffer lengths.
5. MIDI 2.0 values
Pitch 7.9 and 7.25, pitch bend, value scaling, UMP timing, decode UMP words.
6. Windows timestamps
MIDI timestamps to time, the gap between two, FILETIME, .NET and Unix time.
7. Compact overlay
One calculator in a small always-on-top window beside your music app.
8. History
Answers you copied, used or pinned, with search.
