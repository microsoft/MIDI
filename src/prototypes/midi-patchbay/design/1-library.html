1
The tile's switch starts and stops routing. A green edge and the live count show what's routing. Starts automatically is the power mark beside the device count. Double-click a tile, or select Edit, to open the patch in its own window. Several patches can be open at once.
2
Device presence on every tile. "4 devices ready", "1 device missing" (the missing one is dashed red on the map), "Loop found, 1 muted". The same words MIDI Glass uses for its layouts.
3
Show only what matters right now. Routing, Not routing, and Needs attention, which is any patch with a missing device or a loop.
4
New patch and New quick patch move here from today's left pane. A quick patch still asks for two devices and opens with them already connected.
5
Ask an AI assistant and Import patch sit where MIDI Glass has them. Settings (start with Windows, notification area, start patches automatically) stay behind the gear in the title bar.
i
The library is the main window. With Run in notification area on, closing it hides it and routing goes on, like today. The notification area menu still starts and stops each patch.
