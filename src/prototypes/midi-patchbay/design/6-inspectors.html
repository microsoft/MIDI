Connections are simple now. No filter, transform or speed of their own: just where they run, a count, Mute, and a quick way to put a block in the middle.
i
Every block has a name. It starts as the kind of block and can be changed, for example to "Upper keys". The line under the name on the canvas always says what the block does.
i
The throttler keeps room for #1254. The dashed part is not built. When thinning arrives it goes here, as a choice on the block, and Network MIDI and RTP-MIDI can share it.
